About Us

Marines MC came into existence on November 10, 1994, in El Paso, Texas. Marines MC requested permission to use the Eagle, Globe, and Anchor on our colors on August 3, 1995, per MCO 5600.31. Authorization was granted by the Commandant of the Marine Corps on September 26, 1995, with the one stipulation that anything shown in public/advertising be accompanied by the following disclaimer: "Neither the United States Marine Corps nor any other component of the Department of Defense has approved, endorsed, or authorized this activity."

The first national run/meeting was at the Four Corners Rally in Ignacio, CO, in September of 1996, followed by one in Angel Fire, NM, in May 1999. Subsequent runs/meetings have convened at Cripple Creek, CO. A national run/meeting is now held every two years at a location designated by and agreed upon by the Membership.
